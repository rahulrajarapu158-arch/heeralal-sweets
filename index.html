OUR STORY

A Legacy of Taste & Trust

Since 1965, Heeralal Sweet House has been a beloved name in Hanamkonda and Warangal. What started as a small sweet shop has grown into a destination for authentic Bengali sweets, pure ghee mithai, and premium dry fruit delicacies.

Every sweet we make is crafted with the finest ingredients — pure desi ghee, fresh khoya, premium dry fruits, and traditional recipes passed down through generations. Our sugar-free range ensures everyone can enjoy the magic of authentic Indian sweets.
